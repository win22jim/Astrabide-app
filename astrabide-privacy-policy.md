# Privacy Policy for Astrabide

**Effective Date:** September 27, 2026
**Last Updated:** September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Astrabide ("the app", "we", "us") handles your data. Astrabide is a single-player sci-fi tower defense game for iPhone and iPad. The app is published by Jeremy Littlewood ("the developer").

## Summary

**Astrabide does not collect, transmit, or sell any of your personal data. The developer operates no servers and receives nothing from the app.** Your progress, settings and custom maps are stored only on your device. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The only online feature is Apple's Game Center, which is optional and is run by Apple.

If that summary is all you need, you can stop reading here. The rest of this document explains the details.

## 1. What the App Stores on Your Device

Everything Astrabide saves stays on your iPhone or iPad:

- **Your progress** — stars, best scores and best waves for each map and difficulty, Research Points, Tech Tree unlocks, and which enemies and bosses you have unlocked in the Codex. Stored in the app's local preferences (`UserDefaults`).
- **Your settings** — sound effects and music volume, default game speed, auto-advance, and colour-blind mode. Also stored in the app's local preferences.
- **Your custom maps** — maps you build in the Map Creator, saved as files in the app's own Documents folder.
- **A run in progress** — saved after every cleared wave so you can pick it up from **Resume Run**, and cleared when the run is won or lost.

The app does not send any of this anywhere, and the developer cannot see it. Your custom maps are never shared with other players.

Like any app's data, this may be included in your device's own backups (iCloud Backup, or a backup to your computer). Those backups are made and handled by Apple and by you, not by the app.

## 2. What the App Does NOT Collect

The app does **not** collect, transmit, or process any of the following:

- Personal identifiers (name, email address, phone number, etc.)
- Advertising or tracking identifiers (IDFA, device fingerprints, etc.)
- Location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or health data
- Usage analytics, gameplay telemetry, or crash telemetry
- Anything sent to the developer's servers — because the developer operates none

The app contains **no** third-party analytics SDKs, **no** advertising SDKs, **no** crash-reporting SDKs, and **no** tracking of any kind. It asks for **no** device permissions. There is no sign-in and no account.

## 3. Game Center

Astrabide uses Apple's **Game Center** for leaderboards — one for each map at each difficulty. Game Center is optional: the game is fully playable without it.

- If your device is signed in to Game Center, the app submits your run's **total score** to the leaderboard for that map and difficulty when a run ends. Scores from custom maps are never submitted.
- Game Center is operated by **Apple** under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). What other players can see — such as your Game Center nickname and your scores — is controlled by your Game Center settings on your device.
- Through Apple's developer tools, the developer can see leaderboard entries (a player's Game Center nickname and score) in order to manage the leaderboards, such as removing an invalid score. The developer does not export, store, or use this information for anything else.
- To stop taking part, sign out of Game Center in your device's Settings. To have your scores removed from Astrabide's leaderboards, email the developer (see Section 9).

## 4. Crash Reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may provide the developer with anonymized crash reports and usage statistics for the app. That sharing is controlled by you and handled by Apple; the app itself sends nothing.

## 5. Children's Privacy

Astrabide does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. The game has no chat, no user-to-user sharing, no ads, and no purchases.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because the developer has no access to your data, you are in full control of it:

- **Custom maps:** delete any map from **Map Select → Custom Maps** with its trash button.
- **Everything else:** deleting the app removes all of its data from your device (unless you later restore it from a device backup).
- **Game Center scores:** email the developer to have your leaderboard scores removed.

## 7. Security

The app stores your data using standard iOS storage inside the app's sandbox, where other apps cannot read it. It makes no network connections of its own; Game Center traffic goes directly between your device and Apple.
