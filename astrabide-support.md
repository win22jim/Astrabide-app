# Astrabide Support

Welcome to the Astrabide support page. Astrabide is a sci-fi tower defense game for iPhone and iPad: place and upgrade towers beside the alien swarm's route, counter every enemy type, and hold the line through fifty waves and five bosses on each map, then Endless.

If your question isn't answered below, email **brindle-tiered-3m@icloud.com**. The developer reads every message personally.

## Contact

- **Email:** brindle-tiered-3m@icloud.com
- **Response time:** typically within 1–2 business days. Anything that affects your saved progress is prioritized.
- **What to include:** your device model, your iOS or iPadOS version, the app version (shown on the main menu), the map and difficulty you were playing, and what you were doing when the problem happened. Screenshots help.

## How to Play

1. Tap **Play**, choose a map, read the briefing, pick a difficulty and tap **Start Game**.
2. **Drag a tower** from the palette on the left onto an open tile beside the route. You spend credits to build.
3. Tap **▶** in the top bar to launch the next wave. The swarm follows the route toward your base; every enemy that gets through costs base HP.
4. **Tap a placed tower** to see its stats, upgrade it, or sell it.
5. Tap the **eye** button for the **Scouting Report**: exactly what the next wave will send, including a warning before every boss wave.
6. Survive all 50 waves to win. After a win you can keep going in **Endless**.

Kills and cleared waves earn credits. A wave with no breaches earns a bonus.

## Towers

| Tower | Cost | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Pulse Cannon** | 100 | Reliable single-target projectile |
| **Arc Tesla** | 125 | Chain lightning that jumps between enemies. Hits flying enemies |
| **Cryo Beam** | 150 | Slows every enemy in range and damages the closest |
| **Nano Swarm** | 175 | Damages every enemy in range. Detects stealthy enemies |
| **Shield Pylon** | 200 | Boosts the damage and fire rate of nearby towers; pylons stack |
| **Railgun Silo** | 300 | Long-range beam that pierces everything in its line. Hits flying and stealthy enemies |

Every tower has two upgrade paths, **Range** and **Speed**, with three tiers each. You can upgrade both paths on the same tower.

## Know Your Enemy

Some enemies can only be stopped by the right tower:

- **Armored** enemies take half damage. Use heavy single hits.
- **Stealthy** enemies can only be targeted by the Nano Swarm and the Railgun Silo.
- **Flying** enemies can only be targeted by the Arc Tesla and the Railgun Silo.
- **Shielded** enemies carry a shield that regenerates. Out-damage it.
- **Healers** repair nearby enemies. Take them out first.

The **Codex** on the main menu has a full entry for every enemy, tower and boss you have met.

## Bosses

A boss arrives every tenth wave, with a warning beforehand. Each of the five has its own behaviour and changes tactics as it takes damage. In Endless, the bosses return, and they keep getting stronger.

## Stars, Research Points and the Tech Tree

- Winning a map earns **stars**: 3 for finishing with your base at full HP, 2 for at least half, 1 for anything left.
- Each new star earns **Research Points**, multiplied by the difficulty. Replaying for stars you already have doesn't earn more.
- Spend Research Points in the **Tech Tree** on permanent upgrades for every future run: more starting credits, bigger kill rewards, better sell refunds and more base HP.

## Map Creator

Build your own maps from the main menu:

1. **Route Draw:** drag your finger across the grid to lay the route; it snaps to tiles.
2. **Start Point** and **Base:** place where the swarm enters and where your base stands. Both must sit on the route.
3. **Terrain:** add obstacles. **Erase:** remove anything you placed.
4. **Theme:** pick one of seven looks, each with its own music.
5. The status panel tells you when the map is **Ready to save**. The route must connect the start to the base.
6. **Test** the map, then **Save** it with a name and an optional briefing.

Your maps appear under **Map Select → Custom Maps**, playable at any difficulty. Custom maps stay on your device, and they don't award stars or post to leaderboards. To delete one, tap its trash button.

## Saving and Resuming

- Your progress, settings and custom maps are saved automatically on your device.
- A run in progress is saved after **every cleared wave**. If you leave mid-run, **Resume Run** on the main menu picks it up with your towers, upgrades, credits, score and base HP intact, starting from the next wave.
- The saved run is cleared when the run is won or lost.

## Game Center

Astrabide has a Game Center leaderboard for every map at every difficulty. Your run's total score (every credit you earned during the run) is posted when the run ends, whether you win or lose.

- Game Center is optional. To use it, sign in under **Settings → Game Center** on your device.
- Tap **Leaderboards** on the main menu to see the rankings.

## iPhone and iPad

- On **iPhone**, Astrabide plays in landscape.
- On **iPad**, it works in any orientation and in resizable windows (Windowed Apps and Stage Manager). You can rotate or resize mid-battle without losing anything.

## Settings and Accessibility

Open **Settings** from the gear on the main menu, or from the pause menu during a game:

- Separate volume controls for **sound effects** and **music**
- **Default game speed** and **auto-advance** between waves
- **Colour-blind mode**, which labels every tower and enemy with a letter or number so none of them relies on colour alone

## Troubleshooting

- **No sound.** Astrabide plays alongside your own music and follows your device's Ring/Silent switch. Check that silent mode is off and that the volumes in Settings are up.
- **Leaderboards doesn't open.** Your device isn't signed in to Game Center. Sign in under Settings → Game Center, then try again.
- **My score isn't on a leaderboard.** Scores are posted when a run ends, and only on campaign maps; custom maps don't post scores. It can take a few moments to appear.
- **Resume Run isn't showing.** There is no run in progress. The saved run is cleared once a run is won or lost.
- **I deleted and reinstalled the app and my progress is gone.** Progress lives on your device only, so deleting the app deletes it. It comes back only if you restore your device from a backup made before the app was deleted.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does Astrabide cost anything?**
No. It's free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

**Does it need an internet connection?**
No. The game is fully playable offline. Only Game Center leaderboards use the network.

**Is my progress synced between my devices?**
Not at the moment. Progress is stored on each device. It moves to a new device when you restore or transfer from a backup.

**Does the app collect my data?**
No. See the [Privacy Policy](astrabide-privacy-policy.md).

**Can I share my custom maps?**
Not yet. Custom maps stay on the device where you made them.

**I found a bug or have an idea. Where do I send it?**
Email **brindle-tiered-3m@icloud.com**.
